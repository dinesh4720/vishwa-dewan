Skilled Migration to Germany

For the German Consulate, as creative director at Visual Reality Events & Production. Four months and 30 posts on Facebook and Instagram.

Challenge

An awareness campaign on skilled migration needed someone to explain, credibly and on camera, what moving to Germany offers. No suitable speaker was available.

Approach

I created Maya, a guide character who fronts the whole campaign, and built the series around her.

  • Planned the full campaign with the content creator
  • Shot and edited the videos and posters that ran on social
  • Found and filmed testimonial interviews with Indian professionals already living and working in Germany
  • Set the paid promotion budget and allocated spend before committing to the creative
